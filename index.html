Inside the lab

Ideas worth building.

Independent research into efficient AI, new model architectures, and tools that make complex systems easier to work with.

01 / SIGNALS

Wave models. A different direction.

Exploring AI models that use I/Q signals—in-phase and quadrature components—to work with wave-based representations. This is an experimental research direction, with the focus on what these representations could offer learning and reasoning.

Research in progress
02 / EFFICIENCY

Make smaller models do more.

Ternary computing, low-bit models, and post-quantization training are central to the lab's work. The aim is useful reasoning with a smaller footprint, measured against the trade-offs in quality, memory, and speed.

Model development
03 / VISUAL TOOLS

See the system you're building.

AI workflows should be easier to inspect. From model maps to connected flow blocks, the lab is developing ways to see how components fit together and follow the path through a complex system.

Tools in development
04 / AGENTS

Keep the goal in view.

Building tools for AI-assisted projects: keeping track of progress, coordinating work, and checking what actually changed. The focus is practical follow-through, with the user choosing the objective and limits.
