01 / Research & directionOwner & Founder

@CodeMasterCody3D

Cody Dixon

Independent AI researcher & builder

33 · Hueytown, Alabama

Background & journey

I’m Cody, owner and founder of Internectual Labs, an independent AI researcher and builder. I started by overclocking hardware and digging into Linux, learning through forums and trial and error. Getting RuneScape running smoothly on Android through Linux was an early win. Later, I pushed an i7-8086K with a compact 120 mm liquid cooler and tuned mining rigs for efficiency. Those experiments taught me to balance performance, power, and stability.

Technical focus

AI took me from not knowing how to code to building my own projects. Now I’m drawn to the same question that drove my hardware experiments: how much more can we get out of less? I want to shrink AI models while retaining their intelligence, connecting model efficiency with the practical limits of the hardware running them.

Work at the lab

That drive led to TAARDIS-27B, the lab’s full-ternary model project. My TK Integers work explores ternary weight formats and GPU kernels. IQ wave models are more experimental, exploring geometry and interacting waves in place of standard layers. I also build tools: GRIP helps AI-assisted projects keep track of where they left off, and GNU-AI-Flow is a visual workspace for model components and training pipelines.

Personal approach

I love pushing limits, questioning assumed boundaries, and improving things until the results hold up. When something fails, I want to understand why and keep working at it. I value friendly, honest conversation and hearing what people actually care about. The lab grows from that same curiosity: build something, test it, share what you learn, and keep going.

Background & journey

I’m Mohammed, a self-taught developer learning since 2023. I originally wanted to study computer science and am now studying Electronics and Information Processing Engineering. Alongside that, I built my Python and software skills, then moved into machine learning, LLMs, RAG, and agents. I’m early in my professional career, with no company employment yet; my experience comes from building projects and contributing to open source.

Technical focus

I’m interested in reliable LLM applications: tool calling, memory, agent orchestration, and source-grounded RAG with hybrid retrieval and embeddings. My backend work uses Python REST APIs, Flask, PostgreSQL, pgvector, and SQLAlchemy. I’m strengthening my ML fundamentals and model evaluation skills, with testing, debugging, Docker, CI/CD, and Git as part of how I build.

Projects & planned lab work

My projects include AgriMarket AI, combining FAOSTAT production and NASA POWER climate data for Moroccan agricultural intelligence, backend pipelines, forecasting, and LangGraph agents; and CodeAtlas, a codebase intelligence tool using hybrid retrieval and source-grounded answers. My open-source work includes embedded bootloader security, regression tests, and computer vision inference validation.

At Internectual, I want to help test TK Integers, evaluate Qwen quantization, and investigate training stages, checkpoint restoration, reliability, and stage integration. I also want to explore multi-agent reasoning and systematic testing while learning more about quantization, training, distillation, and reasoning optimization. These are intended areas of contribution as I get started.

Personal approach

I learn by implementing ideas, understanding failures, and improving what I build. I care about helping developers, sharing knowledge, and collaborating in open source. I want my work to make a meaningful contribution beyond a line on my CV.
