01 / Research & directionOwner & Founder

@CodeMasterCody3D

Cody Dixon

Efficient AI · Experimental research

I’m Cody, the founder of Internectual Labs. I’m an independent researcher and builder, and most of my work starts with one question: how far can a system be pushed before it breaks?

I got my start overclocking hardware and digging into Linux. That was before AI assistants, so I learned from forum threads and a lot of trial and error. One of my early wins was getting RuneScape to run smoothly on an Android phone through a Linux environment. After that the experiments got bigger. I ran an i7-8086K on a compact 120 mm liquid cooler, and I tuned mining rigs to get more work out of less power. That’s where I learned to balance performance against power draw and stability, and I still work that way.

Now I’m pointing that same instinct at AI. I want to know how small a model can get and still think clearly. TK Integers is my work on ternary weight formats and the GPU kernels that run them. TAARDIS-27B is the lab’s fully ternary model. My IQ wave models are more experimental and try to compute with geometry and interacting waves instead of standard layers. I also build my own tools. GRIP keeps AI-assisted projects from losing track of where they left off. GNU-AI-Flow is a visual workspace for putting together model components and training pipelines.

I like taking ideas most people would skip, checking them against published research, building prototypes, and testing them until the results hold up. When I hit a wall, I want to know why it’s there, and then I look for a way around it. I do this because I love the work, and that’s what Internectual Labs is built on.
